Privacy Policy

Fiber Forms — an internal tool used by fiber installation technicians to fill in job paperwork and take installation photos.
Last updated: 1 September 2026

What it handles

Job details typed in by the technician (customer name, service address, equipment serial numbers, Wi-Fi credentials, optical and speed readings), the customer's on-screen signature, and installation photos.

Where it stays

Fiber Forms runs entirely in the phone's browser. There is no server behind it. Everything is stored on the device and is erased by the app's Clear buttons or by clearing browser data. No analytics, no cookies, no tracking, and nothing is shared or sold.

Google Drive

Files leave the device only when the technician taps Share or Upload to Drive. The upload uses one permission, https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file, which limits the app to files and folders it created itself plus any folder chosen through Google's own picker. It cannot read or delete anything else in the account. Sign-in happens on Google's pages; the app never sees the password.

Fiber Forms' use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
